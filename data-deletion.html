Sanipure CRM Data Deletion Instructions

Customers may request deletion of personal information collected through the Sanipure CRM WhatsApp service.

To request deletion, send an email to:

cs.crm@sanipure.com.eg

Please include your name, WhatsApp phone number, and a request to delete your CRM information.

Sanipure will review and process valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable legal and business requirements.
